About

Biography

I am a PhD candidate in the Research School of Engineering at the Australian National University, supervised by Prof. Thushara Abhayapala, Dr. Jihui (Aimee) Zhang, and Prof. Prasanga Samarasinghe.

My research focuses on audio and acoustic signal processing — in particular, how to reconstruct continuous sound fields from sparse microphone observations, and how to render immersive spatial audio for binaural and surround playback.

In 2025, I interned at Tencent Music Entertainment (QQ Music) in Shenzhen, designing and developing a production spatial audio renderer. I hold a First-Class Honours B.E. from ANU, double-majored in Electronic & Communication Systems and Mechatronic Systems (GPA 6.77/7).
